Peak-DAU subscription按峰值 DAU 订阅

AppPushWebPush

The model: a monthly subscription built on your single highest DAU day of the month. Push volume and registered users are unlimited within the plan.计费方式：按月订阅，计费依据为当月单日最高 DAU（日活跃用户数）。方案内推送数量与注册用户数均不限。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Send frequency is fully decoupled from cost. Broadcast twice as often, re-engage dormant users, run flash sales — your bill doesn't move. There's no disincentive to sending the extra notification that could recover a user.发送频次与费用完全脱钩。无论增加推送频次、召回沉默用户，还是开展限时促销，账单都不会因此变化；多推一条可能唤回用户的通知，不会有任何成本顾虑。
  • Campaign spikes can't inflate the bill. A viral day is already covered by your DAU tier; traffic intensity above your normal level never triggers message-based charges.流量爆发不会推高账单。爆款传播带来的高峰日已包含在 DAU 档位内，超出日常水平的流量不会触发按条计费。
  • You budget against one number. Audience size in, one subscription out — easy to forecast month to month.只需盯住一个数字做预算。按受众规模对应固定订阅费用，逐月预测轻松简单。
  • Capacity costs are transparent. A standard send-through capacity is included; higher throughput is the only typical add-on, priced per fixed increment.容量成本透明。方案已包含标准发送吞吐容量；如需更高吞吐，这通常也是唯一的加购项，按固定档位计价。

Large audience? DAU-tier pricing softens considerably at higher bands — share your peak numbers with us.受众规模较大？DAU 档位单价在更高区间会显著优化——欢迎把您的峰值数据告诉我们。

Prepaid volume packs预付费套餐包

Email

The model: buy a pack of emails up front; it holds its value for 24 months, and unused emails don't expire at month end.计费方式：一次性购买邮件套餐包，余额有效期长达 24 个月，未使用的邮件不会在月底清零。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • No use-it-or-lose-it pressure. Unlike monthly quotas, unused balance carries for up to two years — seasonal senders never forfeit paid emails.没有“不用即作废”的压力。与月度配额不同，未使用余额最长可结转两年——业务有季节性也不会浪费已购邮件。
  • Unit price drops as packs get bigger. Buying ahead of a known campaign is genuinely cheaper than topping up as you go.套餐越大，单价越低。在已知大型营销活动前提前购买，比随用随充更划算。
  • Add-ons are unbundled and optional. Dedicated IPs, extra data, authentication, longer storage, inbox previews and insights are each priced separately — you never subsidize features you don't use.增值项全部解耦、按需选择。专属 IP、额外流量、认证服务、更长存储、进箱预览与数据分析等均单独计价，无需为用不上的功能买单。
  • Free starting tier. Every new account gets 50 free emails per day.提供免费起步额度。每个新账户每天可免费发送 50 封邮件。

Sending millions a month? Enterprise volume rates and custom packages are available.月发送量达数百万级？可享企业级量大单价与定制套餐。

Pure pay-as-you-go纯按量付费

SMS

The model: one unit price per message, set by destination country — no monthly floor, no platform fee.计费方式：按条单价计费，单价按目的地国家 / 地区确定——无月度最低消费，无平台费。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Slow months cost almost nothing. With no fixed fee, a quiet period shrinks your bill instead of leaving a platform charge on the books.业务淡季几乎零成本。没有固定费用，发送量低的月份账单随之缩减，不会留下一笔平台费支出。
  • Simple to model and approve. Unit price × estimated messages is the entire calculation — no buried line items or reconciliation.核算与审批简单。单价 × 预估发送量即为全部费用，没有隐藏收费项，也无需对账。
  • No upfront gamble. You can enter a new market or run a test campaign without pre-committing to a package.无需前期投入即可尝试。进入新市场或开展测试活动，不必预先承诺购买套餐。
  • Cost follows your actual routes. Each destination has its own unit price, so the bill reflects where messages really went rather than a blended average.费用与实际路由一致。每个目的地对应独立单价，账单如实反映短信实际触达的地区，而非统一混合均价。

High monthly volume? Unit pricing comes down as volume goes up — talk to us about your routes and traffic mix.月发送量较高？单价随量级阶梯下探——联系我们，告知您的路由与流量结构。

All-in pay-as-you-go一口价按量付费

WhatsApp

The model: one unit price per message by country and message category, already covering the official Meta platform fee plus our service fee — no separate platform fee.计费方式：按国家 / 地区与消息类别按条一口价计费，单价已包含 Meta 官方平台费与我们的服务费——无需另行支付平台费。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • No second invoice. The official platform fee is already inside the unit price — there is nothing extra to reconcile with the channel.不会收到第二张账单。官方平台费已包含在单价内，无需再与渠道方单独对账。
  • Quiet months shrink to nothing. There's no fixed fee or floor — billing simply follows your actual sends.发送淡季费用自然趋近于零。没有固定费用或最低消费，账单完全跟随实际发送量。
  • Every market is covered. Standard unit prices span around 140 markets, and markets outside the list still have a defined fallback rate — never an unpriced route.市场覆盖无死角。标准单价覆盖约 140 个市场，列表之外的市场也设有明确的兜底费率，绝不会出现无法计价的路由。
  • Easy to forecast and approve. Country × category unit price × expected messages is the whole calculation; testing a new market needs no pre-commitment.易于预测与审批。国家 / 地区 × 消息类别的单价 × 预期发送量即为完整测算；测试新市场无需任何前期承诺。

Running large WhatsApp programs? Unit rates are negotiable based on monthly volume and category mix — talk to us.WhatsApp 业务规模较大？单价可根据月发送量与消息类别结构协商——联系我们。

Pay only for success仅为成功付费

OTPSilent Auth

The model: you are charged only when a verification succeeds. Failed, timed-out, or unverified attempts cost nothing.计费方式：仅在验证成功时计费，验证失败、超时或未完成的尝试均不收费。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Zero cost of failure. Every billable event is a completed verification — a user actually got in. There is no spend on attempts that delivered nothing.失败零成本。每一次计费都对应一次完成的验证——用户确实完成了登录或注册，不会为没有结果的尝试付费。
  • Cost self-justifies. More charges can only mean more verified logins, so finance never sees a bill disconnected from an outcome.费用本身即价值证明。计费次数增长只可能意味着成功验证的用户增多，财务团队看到的每一笔账单都对应明确的业务结果。
  • No volume to forecast. You never pre-buy a verification package that might go unused; billing simply follows your real success count.无需预测用量。不必预先购买可能闲置的验证套餐，计费自然跟随实际成功次数。
  • Safe to add as a supplement. Running it alongside another verification channel can't generate wasted spend on failures.作为补充通道也可放心接入。与其他验证方式并行使用，不会因失败尝试产生无效支出。

Sending at scale? Per-verification rates are negotiable based on your monthly volume.发送量级较大？每次验证的单价可根据月量级协商。

Per-engaged-user subscription按互动用户订阅

Marketing Automation营销自动化（MA）

The model: priced on Monthly Engaged Persons — unique users who enter any journey that month, de-duplicated per project — plus a fixed platform fee.计费方式：按 MEP（月度互动用户数）计费——当月进入任意旅程的去重独立用户（按项目去重），另加固定平台服务费。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Sophistication doesn't multiply cost. One person in five journeys counts once. Adding touchpoints, channels or frequency inside journeys never raises the per-person price.编排复杂度不会成倍放大成本。同一用户进入五条旅程也只计一次；在旅程内增加触达节点、渠道或频次，都不会抬高单人单价。
  • Generous event headroom. Every block of engaged users includes a large monthly event allowance, so standard lifecycle tracking doesn't trigger extra charges. Only defined custom, recommended or registration events count; standard system events are free.事件额度充裕。每个互动用户档位都包含大量月度事件额度，常规生命周期追踪不会产生额外费用；仅指定的自定义事件、推荐事件或注册事件计入，标准系统事件免费。
  • The platform fee is fixed and complete. It covers all platform features, AI value-adds, a monthly AI credit grant, journey design and delivery, and 24/7 support — no feature-gated upsells.平台服务费固定且完整。涵盖全部平台功能、AI 增值能力、每月 AI 积分赠送、旅程设计与投放，以及 7×24 小时支持，没有功能解锁式的二次销售。
  • Stable audience, stable bill. If your addressable base is steady, cost stays predictable even as campaigns change.受众稳定，账单即稳定。可触达用户基数平稳时，即使营销活动变化，费用依然可预期。

Big engaged-user base? Per-person rates are negotiable as your MEP scales.互动用户基数较大？单人单价可随 MEP 量级协商。

Flat subscription, unlimited seats固定订阅，席位不限

LiveDesk

The model: fixed monthly plans with no per-agent charges.计费方式：固定月度方案，不按客服坐席收费。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Headcount decisions stay free. Adding agents, night-shift coverage, or scaling for a launch costs nothing extra — the model never penalizes staffing the service level customers expect.人员配置决策完全自由。增加客服人手、夜班覆盖，或为产品发布扩容，都不会产生额外费用——计费方式不会阻碍您配置客户期望的服务人力。
  • Fully predictable line item. One flat price per month; ticket surges within your plan don't change it.费用完全可预期。每月一笔固定费用；方案范围内的工单高峰不会改变价格。
  • Flex without a plan jump. If conversations temporarily exceed your plan, you can add them in small blocks instead of being forced onto the next tier.临时超量无需跳档。对话量临时超出方案时，可按小额加购包补充，而非被迫升级到更高一档。
  • All channels at every tier. All nine support channels and a monthly AI credit grant are included even on the entry plan.各档位均开放全部通道。即便入门方案，也包含全部 9 条服务通道与每月 AI 积分赠送。

High conversation volume? Custom plans and extra conversation blocks are available at negotiated rates.对话量较高？可享定制方案与额外对话加购包，单价可协商。

Annual peak-capacity plan年度峰值容量方案

CAPTCHA

The model: an annual plan sized to your peak hourly verification requests, sold per organization ID.计费方式：按峰值每小时验证请求量确定的年度方案，以组织 ID 为单位购买。

Why the model works in your favor这种计费方式对您的好处

  • Sized to your real peak, not average traffic. The plan covers the worst hour you actually face, so launch days and traffic surges are covered without overpaying for day-to-day volume.按真实峰值而非平均流量定档。方案覆盖实际面临的最高一小时，产品发布日与流量高峰均有保障，同时无需为日常流量过度付费。
  • Attack traffic inside your tier costs nothing extra. Request spikes from bots don't turn into per-request charges — capacity is pre-locked for the year.档位内的攻击流量不额外收费。机器人带来的请求突增不会变成按次计费，容量在一年内预先锁定。
  • Tiers make right-sizing mechanical. Pick the hourly tier above your peak; higher tiers add scenario IDs and throughput in step.档位让选型变得简单。选择高于自身峰值的每小时档位即可；更高档位同步增加场景 ID 与吞吐能力。
  • One annual budget cycle. A single yearly commitment replaces ongoing usage tracking and variance.一年一次预算周期。单笔年度承诺取代持续的用量跟踪与差异核对。

Peaks beyond standard tiers? Plans can be customized to your traffic profile.峰值超出标准档位？可根据您的流量特征定制方案。
